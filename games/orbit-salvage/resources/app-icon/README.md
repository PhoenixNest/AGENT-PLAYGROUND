## Technical Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 1024 × 1024 pixels (1:1 aspect ratio)
- **Format:** PNG
- **Color Profile:** sRGB IEC61966-2.1
- **Color Mode:** 24-bit RGB (fully opaque, 0 alpha channels)
- **Geometry:** Square canvas with sharp 90° corners (iOS automatically applies the squircle mask at runtime)
- **Safe Area:** All primary visual interest and focal silhouettes reside inside the central ~80% safe zone (`x, y ∈ [102, 922]`), ensuring no cropping under iOS mask or home screen notification badge overlays.
- **App Store Compliance:** No typography or text, no Apple/Google trademarks or logos, no copyrighted imagery, and no Apple Unicode emoji glyph artwork.

## Why `orbital-salvage-icon-1024.png` is the Recommended Primary

1. **Immediate Focal Readability:** The centered spherical mascot with glossy, expressive chibi eyes instantly establishes emotional connection on the App Store grid and device home screen.
2. **Iconic Silhouette at Small Sizes:** When scaled down to 60×60 pt on iOS Springboard or 29×29 pt in Settings, the pink glowing ring around the dark navy sphere provides an instantly recognizable high-contrast mark.
3. **Thematic Harmony:** Balances cosmic themes (black hole + glowing accretion ring) with playful arcade salvage elements (floating donut, golden star, radiant cyan gem).
