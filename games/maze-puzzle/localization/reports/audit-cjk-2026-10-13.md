## Playfield font

Gate labels, the ring hub, and the floating words go through `Canvas2D.FillText` with a Fredoka or Nunito CSS font and no `TextChain`. English still leaves the chain null. Any other language now resolves `OutlineFonts.ChainFor` for that family. Middle baseline uses the Latin face's metrics so the badge center stays where the English word sat. `GameplayText_UsesTheCjkFace_EnglishStaysOnFredoka` checks all four codes and then `BOOST` on Fredoka.
