### 3. “Try Again” is not German

`modal_defeat_12_of_20` and `modal_defeat_no_backup_low`. The yellow button is `Try Again` → `Versuch's`. That apostrophe-s is English grammar. The house icon and the word both sit in the button; the word is fully visible and is still wrong.

Suggested: `Nochmal` (already used for Play Again on the win modal).

`Hangar` → `Heim` fits and reads as home. `Lost In Void` → `In der Leere` reads as the spilled-mass stat, which is the right reading on this card.

### 4. Unlocked trophies say “open”

`achievements_all` and `achievements_some`. `✓ UNLOCKED` → `✓ OFFEN`. On a trophy that is already earned, “offen” reads as still open, not as owned. `CLAIMED ✓` → `ABGEHOLT ✓` is a different string and is not this badge.

Suggested badge: `✓ DABEI`.

`LOCKED` → `GESPERRT` is the right word and shrinks to 0.78 of 12px = 9.4px inside a 50px badge (five badges on `achievements_none`, three on `achievements_some`, both profiles). The still still reads it. Shorter badge if 9.4px is too small: `SPERRE`.

## Strings that stayed English

Intentional copies, and they should stay:

- Brand runs: `ORBITAL`, `SALVAGE`, `ORBITAL SALVAGE`, `SPACE TRASH CLEAN`, `Orbital Salvage • Space Trash Clean v1.2.0`.
- Pure `kg` quotas (`0 / 20 kg`, `12 / 20 kg`, `20 kg`, and the rest of that set).
- Symbol rows with no new letters: `fx.gate.zap` `⚡ ×{0}`, `fx.gate.shred` `💥 ×{0}!`, `fx.gate.magnet` `🧲 +{0}`, `fx.hub.orbital` `✨ ORBITAL ✨`, `hud.sector_code` `⭐ {0}`.
- The settings value `English`. That run is the endonym of the language saved in the capture. `Language` itself is `Sprache`. These stills do not move the `✓` off `English`, because `PagePreviewPainter` paints the captured marker. The app moves it when `de` is the saved language. I did not paint that state.

Loanwords inside German sentences, left on purpose by the translator: `Nibbles`, `Level`, `Donut`, `Bronze`, `Cyber`, `Background` in `Leise Background-Melodie`, `localStorage`, `Smoothie`. Umlauts in the same lines render (`Trophäen`, `Zurück`, `für`, `größte`, `Müll`, `Käse`). I saw no `.notdef` box on a German letter. I saw no `ß`, and no `ß`→`SS`, because no uppercased string in this table contains `ß`.

Not a missing key, and it looks like leftover English:

- `game_lvl01_t1000ms` draws the HUD in German (`Level 1`, `0 / 20 kg`) and the ring in English (`DONUT`, `SWEET RIG`, `×2! SHRED`). Those ring words are pixels in the parity playfield recording. They do not go through `Loc`. The live renderer does. `playfield-live.png` shows `MAMPF!`, `HAMM!`, `SCHLUCK!`, `LECKER!`, `×5! FETZEN`, `+7! DICHT`, `SCHUB!`, `PUFF!`, `FLOTT!`, hub `Donut` / `SÜSS-RING`, and gate badges `⚡ ×2` / `🧲 +2` / `⏩ SCHUB`. `Donut` is the deliberate case change of `🍩 DONUT 🍩` so the loanword would pass the untranslated check. `fx.hub.orbital` stays `✨ ORBITAL ✨`.

The Khmer, Hindi, and Arabic buttons on the language picker are tofu boxes. That is the missing-font gap already called for those three languages, not a German glyph hole. `Deutsch`, `Tiếng Việt`, and `Oʻzbekcha` draw. `Zu` fits the 32px close control.

## What the render hook cannot do for German

The Latin breaker splits on spaces only. It does not hyphenate. A compound that is one run (`Taschengeld`, `Kinderschutz`, `Horizontsektor`) cannot break in the middle.

`Taschengeld.` survived because the English capture already put `allowance.` in its own run, under `Hol dir gratis +100`. The shop banner shows both lines, and the word fits. That is not hyphenation.

On the map and shop cards the long compounds the translator feared do fit at full size in these stills: `Kaugummi-Rutsche`, `Horizontsektor`, `Nibbles-Hüte`, `Käsemond-Bahn`, `Singular-Horizont`, `Kessler-Singularität`. The fit warnings were tighter than the painted boxes.

Sibling elements stay at the captured y. A wrapped paragraph does not push the next heading. That is why the privacy modal collides. Shrinking and ellipsis keep a run inside its own box, which is what the audit measures, and they do not fix two boxes that already share a top.

## Verdict

German chrome is in good shape on home, loading, maps, shop, pause, debrief, trophies, and the settings rows. Umlauts render. The compounds that scared the fit checker fit on the real cards. I would not ship the privacy modal or the daily counter pill as they are. `Versuch's` and `✓ OFFEN` are copy bugs, not engine bugs. The game-page ring English is the old recording, not the table; live play is German.

## Not verified

No device, no native speaker, no profile with `settings.language` saved as `de` (so the picker check stays on the captured English). Toasts other than the two debrief banners were not on a still; their strings are in the table and were not measured for width (`str` rows have no box). Gate labels were checked on one live level-1 frame, not on every sector.
